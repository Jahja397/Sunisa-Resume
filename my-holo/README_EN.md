# MYHOLO ✦ Y2K Space

[อ่านภาษาไทย](README_TH.md)

MYHOLO is a social chat website with a Y2K look: pastel gradients, pixel fonts and sparkles floating in the background. You make your own page, hang out in live chat rooms, add friends and talk to them privately. Everything updates in real time, so new messages, friend requests and online statuses show up instantly without refreshing.

This document explains everything you can do on MYHOLO, page by page.

---

## The main pages

After logging in, the menu at the top takes you to five places:

| Menu | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Your greeting card, how many people are online, a button to turn on notifications, and shortcuts to the other pages |
| **Chat** | The list of 9 public chat rooms |
| **People** | Search for people by username or ID |
| **My Page** | Your profile, your friends and the people you've blocked |
| **Inbox** | Friend requests and your private chats |

The red badge on **Inbox** counts your unread chats plus new friend requests, and the browser tab shows the same number, for example *(2) MYHOLO*.

---

## 1. Creating an account

- **Sign up** with a username, an email and a password. The password needs at least 6 characters.
- **Username rules**: 3–20 characters, using English letters, numbers, `_` or Thai. A name can't start with a Thai vowel or tone mark that sits above or below a letter (like ั or ่), and every username is unique.
- **Email verification**: after signing up, MYHOLO sends a link to your email. You can't use the site until you click it. The *Check your email* screen notices the click on its own within a few seconds, or you can press **I've verified ✓**.
- **Didn't get the email?** Check Spam or Promotions, or press **Send the link again** (you can resend once a minute). If you typed the wrong email, press **Wrong email? Start over** to cancel that account and sign up again.
- **Your username is reserved after you verify.** If someone else took that name while you were verifying, MYHOLO asks you to choose another one.
- **Your ID**: every account gets a random ID made of 2 letters and 4 numbers, like `#KX2048`. The letters I and O are never used, so they can't be confused with 1 and 0.

## 2. Logging in and out

- **Log in with your email and password.** Logging in with a username isn't possible, which keeps everyone's email private.
- **Forgot your password?** Type your email on the login screen and press **Forgot password?**. A link to set a new password is sent to that email.
- **Old accounts that never verified** see a *Verify your email* screen first, with a button that sends the verification link.
- **If your page goes missing** (for example, it was deleted by mistake), your account still works. When you log in, a *Restore your page* screen fills in your old username so you can bring your page back with one click. You get a new ID, your colors reset, your old messages are still there, and friends need to be added again.
- **Log out** with **[Logout]** in the menu. Logging out in one tab also logs out every other tab in the same browser.

## 3. Home

- A greeting card with your avatar, your username, your ID and your online status, plus a button that goes to My Page.
- **Turn on notifications**: one tap asks the browser for permission. MYHOLO never asks by itself.
- **Live & Real-time** shows how many people are online right now.
- A reminder that every chat shows its latest 80 messages.
- Shortcuts to World Chat, Inbox and Find People.

## 4. Chat rooms

There are 9 public rooms, and anyone with a verified account can join:

| Room | Theme |
| --- | --- |
| #GlobalChat | Main hub |
| #Y2K_Nostalgia | Old vibes |
| #GamingHub | Pixel play |
| #MusicLounge | Beats |
| #ArtGallery | Art space |
| #MeetFriends | Social |
| #DatingRoom | Love space |
| #LGBTQ+ | Pride |
| #HelpCenter | Support |

Inside a room you can:

- **Read messages live.** Each message shows the sender's name, their ID and the time. A ♡ next to a name means that person is your friend.
- **Send messages** with **Enter** or the **Send** button. A message can be up to 1,000 characters.
- **Add emoji** with the 😀 button, which opens a panel of 116 emoji. The emoji goes wherever your cursor is. The panel closes when you send, tap somewhere else, or press Esc. On phones, picking emoji doesn't pop up the keyboard every time.
- **Send photos** with the 🖼️ button. Any image up to 10 MB works. MYHOLO shrinks it to at most 900 pixels and compresses it before sending, and transparent parts turn white instead of black.
- **Delete your own messages** with 🗑. On a computer it appears when you hover over your message; on a phone it's always faintly visible. You'll be asked to confirm.
- **See which day messages are from.** Labels like *Today*, *Yesterday* or a date separate the days.
- **Scroll back freely.** The chat only jumps to new messages if you're already at the bottom, so reading older messages isn't interrupted.
- **Click anyone's name** to open their profile.
- Messages from people you've blocked are hidden.

## 5. Finding people

On the **People** page you can search in two ways:

- **By the start of a username**, in English or Thai. For example, typing `be` finds `beam`.
- **By full ID**, with or without the `#`, like `#KX2048` or `KX2048`.

Each result shows the person's avatar, ID and online status, plus a button that depends on your relationship:

| Button | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **+ Add Friend** | Send a friend request |
| **Requested ✓** | You've already sent a request |
| **Accept ♡** | They've sent you a request; tap to accept |
| **Chat ♡** | You're already friends; opens your private chat |
| **Blocked** | You've blocked this person |
| **You ✦** | That's you |

Click a result to open the full profile. If you come back with the Back button, your search results are still there.

## 6. Other people's profiles

A profile shows the person's avatar, username, ID, how many friends they have, whether they're online (or when they were last seen), their caption and their bio. The buttons change depending on your relationship:

- **Not friends yet**: **+ Add Friend** and **Block**.
- **You sent a request**: *Request sent ✓* with **Cancel request**.
- **They sent you a request**: **Accept ♡** or **Decline**.
- **Friends**: **Chat ♡** and **Unfriend**.
- **Blocked**: **Unblock**.

## 7. Friends

- **Clicking a name never adds a friend by accident.** A friendship only starts when you send a request and the other person accepts it.
- **When someone sends you a request**, a pop-up appears with a **View** button, and the Inbox badge goes up by one.
- **If they've already sent you a request**, their profile and search result show **Accept ♡** instead of **+ Add Friend**, so you never end up with two requests.
- **When your request is accepted**, you get a pop-up saying so, and that person appears in your Inbox and on My Page.
- **Unfriend** removes the friendship for both of you.
- **Block** removes the friendship, cancels any requests, stops that person from sending you requests or private messages, and hides their messages in chat rooms for you. You can unblock anyone from their profile or from My Page.

## 8. Private chat

- Private chats are **only between friends**, and only the two of you can read them.
- You get the same tools as in chat rooms: emoji, photos, deleting your own messages and day labels.
- The top bar shows your friend's name (click it to see their profile) and whether they're online.
- **Opening a chat marks it as read** on all your devices, so the unread dot disappears on your phone and your computer.
- If your friend unfriends you while the chat is open, the message box changes to a notice that you're not friends anymore.
- If a friendship only exists on one side (from an older version of MYHOLO), the chat explains that the other person hasn't added you back yet and offers a **Send request** button.

## 9. Inbox

- **Friend requests** appear at the top, each with **Accept ♡** and **Decline**.
- **Your chats** are listed underneath, with the most recent conversation first. Each row shows your friend's avatar and online dot, their name, a preview of the last message (*You:* when it's yours, 📷 *Image* for photos) and the time or date.
- **Unread chats** have a pink dot and a bold name.

## 10. Notifications

- **Pop-ups inside MYHOLO** appear when a friend messages you (with an **Open** button), when someone sends you a friend request (with **View**), and when someone accepts your request.
- **Browser notifications**, once turned on, appear when you're not looking at the MYHOLO tab. Clicking one opens that chat.
- **On phones**, pop-ups appear at the top of the screen so they never cover the message box.

## 11. My Page and editing your profile

- **My Page** shows your avatar, username, ID, caption and bio. Your avatar uses the first letter of your username, and Thai names skip floating vowel and tone marks so the letter looks right.
- **Friends** are shown as a grid. Click a friend to open your chat with them.
- **Blocked** lists the people you've blocked, each with an **Unblock** button. This section only appears if you've blocked someone.
- **Edit Profile** lets you:
  - pick **two colors** for your avatar's gradient,
  - write a **caption** of up to 100 characters,
  - write a **bio** of up to 500 characters, with line breaks kept.

## 12. Online status

- A **green dot** means the person has MYHOLO open on at least one device or tab.
- A **grey dot** means they're offline, with *last seen* showing the time (if it was today) or the date.
- Using several tabs or devices at once is handled correctly. You only go offline when the last one closes or logs out.

## 13. Links, refresh and the Back button

- **Every page has its own link**, for example `#/room/global` for a chat room, `#/inbox` for the Inbox, or `#/u/...` for someone's profile.
- **Refreshing keeps you on the same page**, and the browser's Back and Forward buttons move between pages inside MYHOLO.
- **If you open a link before logging in**, you're taken to that page right after you log in.

## 14. Phones and Thai

- The layout fits phone screens, tested down to 320 pixels wide, and nothing gets cut off.
- Thai works everywhere: usernames, messages, captions and bios. Thai text uses the Chakra Petch font to match the pixel style.

## 15. Safety and privacy

- **A real email is required.** Accounts with made-up emails can't verify, so they can't read or post anything.
- **Your email is never shown** to other users.
- **Nobody can pretend to be you.** Messages always show the sender's real username and ID.
- **Private chats stay private.** Only the two friends in a chat can read it.
- **Nobody can edit your profile** except you.
- **Message times come from the server**, so messages stay in the right order even if someone's clock is wrong.
- **Blocking** stops unwanted requests and messages.

## Good to know

- Each chat shows its latest 80 messages.
- Search matches the beginning of a username, or a full ID.
- Messages can be deleted but not edited.
- Photos are the only kind of file you can send.
- Browser notifications work while MYHOLO is open in a tab.
